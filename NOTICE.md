# Source and asset notices

The GLOM–TGITO implementation and experiment scripts are provided by the project authors. No blanket licence is applied here to third-party data, software or pretrained models.

Third-party code retains its upstream notices in `experiment/third_party/` and `biophysics/tools/SPURS/`. The public-model download manifest links each model to its upstream repository and fixed revision. Consult those sources for model-specific terms.

The GFP assay data originate from Gonzalez Somermeyer et al. (2022). Its source repository states GPL-3.0; the original licence is preserved in `docs/licenses/Orthologous_GFP_Fitness_Peaks_GPL-3.0.txt`. The source citation and exact download location are recorded in `docs/DATA.md`.

The experimental structural reference is PDB 2G3O. AmberTools/OpenMM dependencies and chromophore parameters remain subject to their respective upstream terms. Environment specifications are provided; their installed distributions are not copied into the repository.
